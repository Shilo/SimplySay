The spreadsheet should mark a pair eligible only when **every requirement passes independently**:

- Exactly two different crates, with at most one of each.
- At least **30 kits**.
- At least **20 mats**.
- Total price **no more than $180**.
- **Both crates confirmed available by Friday**, before Saturday’s event.

Use an `AND` rule, not a score that lets one strength cancel a failure. Kits and mats serve different needs: extra kits cannot fill a mat shortage, and extra mats cannot fill a kit shortage. Likewise, enough supplies at a good price do not help if their availability before the event is unconfirmed. “Tentative” must count as failing the confirmation check.
