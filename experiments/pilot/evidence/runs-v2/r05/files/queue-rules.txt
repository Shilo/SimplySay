This is a toy notification queue. Use only these rules.
A stored job has a stable job_id and one state: ready, leased, or done.
A worker leases a ready job for 30 seconds. A lease that expires before the job is done makes that job available to another worker.
A worker sends a notice to a separate receiver and then marks the stored job done. These are separate operations; there is no transaction spanning them.
A worker may stop at any point, and an old worker may continue running after its lease expires.
A send request may time out even if the receiver accepted it. The sender cannot infer acceptance from a timeout.
In the current design the receiver does not deduplicate requests.
We do not yet know whether the receiver can be changed to support durable idempotency keys.
Jobs can be retried. Eventual delivery requires that retries continue and some attempt eventually succeeds.
For this project a missed notice is worse than a duplicate, but duplicates should be prevented where the rules allow.
