**An offline green tick does not guarantee that the person is currently allowed in. And two offline phones can accept the same ticket twice.**

According to [scanner-notes.md](<TASK_ROOT>/scanner-notes.md), green means the ticket’s signed details check out, its time window matches the phone’s clock, and the ticket is absent from that phone’s cancellation list and used-ticket list. Those lists can be out of date, and the clock can be wrong.

The examples show both problems:

- **Canceled ticket T-41:** canceled at 09:10, after both phones downloaded their cancellation lists at 09:00. At 10:00, **both phones show green**, even though it is canceled.
- **Expired ticket T-99:** expired at 09:59. At the real time of 10:00, phone A rejects it, but phone B shows green because its clock reads 09:58.

Each offline phone remembers only tickets accepted on that phone. It blocks a repeat scan locally, but the other phone can still accept the same ticket. Green includes no separate warning about outdated cancellation information.

**For reliable duplicate prevention, every admission must use the shared online check-in described in the notes.** It checks current cancellations and records ticket use in one operation, so only one competing scan succeeds. Reconnecting afterward merely uploads offline records; it cannot prevent or undo duplicate admissions.

These notes describe a demo prototype, not a live service.
