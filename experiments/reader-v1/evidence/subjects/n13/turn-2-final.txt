**Use both:** the shared folder for handing over current working files, and the separate backup for recovering from mistakes. I’d treat recovery as unverified until someone tests it.

Before the handover, check:

- **Current files and access:** Make sure the shared folder contains everything needed and the next coordinator can open and update it using their own account.
- **A working restore:** Ask whoever handles backups to restore a sample file into a separate folder and open it. A “successful” backup job alone doesn’t establish that recovery works.
- **Coverage and dates:** Confirm the backup includes the needed files, when the latest usable copy was made, and that earlier copies are available for the stated 30 days. Daily backups may miss changes made since the last run.
- **Recovery instructions:** Give the next coordinator a short written procedure, including whom to contact and what access they need.

Until those checks are done, the shared folder supports the handover, but recovery of a deleted file remains uncertain.
