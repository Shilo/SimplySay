Reconciliation groups rows by `record_id`, then chooses the highest numeric revision within each group.

Consider these rows arriving in order:

| Arrival | record_id | revision | title | status | owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | W-9 | 2 | Paint benches | open | Ari |
| 2 | W-3 | 1 | Paint benches | queued | Bo |
| 3 | W-9 | 12 | Paint benches | assigned | Cy |
| 4 | W-9 | 12 | Paint benches | closed | Dee |

After arrivals 1 and 2, **both work orders survive**. Their different IDs identify separate orders, despite the shared title. W-3’s revision is compared only with other W-3 revisions.

Arrival 3 replaces W-9’s revision 2 with revision 12. The **entire third row wins**, including `status: 'assigned'` and `owner: 'Cy'`. Fields present only in the older row would disappear; the merger copies the winning row rather than combining fields.

Arrival 4 ties the current highest revision. It is ignored: the first encountered revision-12 row remains the winner, so Cy stays the owner.

The final output is:

1. W-9, revision 12, assigned to Cy.
2. W-3, revision 1, queued with Bo.

Output order follows each ID’s **first appearance**. The implementation uses a `Map`; replacing an existing key’s value preserves its position. There is no title sorting, so later revisions and renamed titles cannot move an order.

The tests catch the earlier mistakes in several ways:

- **Shared titles:** require both distinct IDs to survive.
- **Numeric revisions:** compare revisions 0, 2, 9, and 12 in different encounter orders, exposing string comparisons and choosing the wrong revision.
- **Ties:** require the first row at the winning revision to survive, including when that revision replaced an earlier one.
- **Complete rows:** require older-only fields to disappear, catching accidental field blending.
- **Stable order and renamed titles:** assert the exact output sequence and one row per ID.
- **Supplied fixture and CLI:** compare complete expected output. Checking only the row count would miss the original result, which dropped W-9 but returned W-3 twice.
