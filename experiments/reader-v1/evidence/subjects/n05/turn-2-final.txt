I’d use **both**: the shared folder to hand over the current working files, and the separate backup to recover from mistakes or loss. The backup is still unproven because nobody has tested a restore.

Before relying on this arrangement, check:

- **Access and completeness:** Can the next coordinator open the shared folder and find all the files they need? Have the person responsible for backups confirm those files are included.
- **Recovery works:** Ask that person to restore a recent file and an older version into a separate location, then open them and check their contents. A “successful” dashboard message alone isn’t enough.
- **Timing meets your needs:** Establish the latest usable backup date. Daily backups can miss changes made since the last run, and copies older than 30 days expire.
- **The handover includes recovery instructions:** Give the next coordinator a named contact, simple steps for requesting recovery, and any access they need.

You can coordinate these checks without managing the backup system yourself.
