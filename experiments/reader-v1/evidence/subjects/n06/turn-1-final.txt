No. According to your notes, deleting a file in the shared folder also deletes it from the other synced laptops. **That does not by itself erase the separate backup**, which is stored outside the shared folder.

The backup runs daily and keeps earlier copies for 30 days. A deleted file may be recoverable if the backup captured it before deletion and that copy has not expired. Changes made since the last backup could be missing.

The uncertainty is whether recovery actually works: recent backup jobs reported success, but nobody has tested restoring a file. Your practical next step is to ask whoever manages backups to **restore a sample file** and give coordinators a clear contact and instructions for recovering missing files. If something is already missing, ask promptly because older copies expire.
