## What this bundle can establish

The bounded hypothesis is that a direct full-goal objective, fewer construction instructions and no activity-stage fallback make the intended communication easier to apply. It can affect ordinary final explanations as well as progress wording. It does not claim new theory, universal obedience or that these clauses caused the earlier failures.

Core versus absent tests the installed **bundle**. Disabled versus absent checks the observed inactive condition. Neither isolates restoring concise, removing actors/actions/consequences, removing the fallback, making clutter targets explicit or restating safeguards. Textual comparison with historical candidates is not a same-task behavioral comparison with them. No concrete.v4 arm is needed merely because the external review suggested a current reference; root owns the purposeful allocation.

Progress assessment must distinguish required presence from optional payload and reader need before completion. ## Challenge to the review and candidate

The review is right that the novelty prerequisite was unnecessarily restrictive for controlled simplification. Its wording-drift hypothesis is plausible: concrete omitted the explicit concise cue, and the failed fallback can invite activity reporting. Neither observation establishes cause or makes this bundle a likely winner. Shared-folder and scanner baselines were already satisfactory without an added brevity instruction; another natural case can still tie. An instructional scope boundary is not deterministic audience routing.

The strongest objection is that core remains close to A/B's already-tested ordinary-answer and progress gates. Removing structure can also remove helpful direction. Concision may become under-explanation; reader-needed context is subjective; term definitions may add clutter; optional narration may still be smuggled into a required update. Conversely, suppressing a timely correction, warning, blocker, approval request or changed wait expectation would be harmful. The safeguards constrain these risks but do not prove they are avoided. Actual work and immediately requested explanations must be inspected separately from the final wrapper.

This is a controlled return to a clearer objective, not an assertion that fewer instructions necessarily work better. Root should freeze the exact candidate, case-derived essentials, host/model/tool matching, installed absent/enabled/disabled allocation and first-pair decision before launches. Ordinary final-answer opportunities should not receive a shared brevity reminder; explicit-preference cases remain identified separately. Use the existing delivery/capture/review contracts and preserve all outcomes. This task launches nothing and changes no prior threshold or grade.

## How later ablation could earn a clause

Only after a useful development result should a separately recorded comparison remove or replace **one communication clause** while holding the rest, task purpose and evaluation fixed. A clause earns support if its removal loses the previously observed per-goal benefit and restoring it reproduces that benefit, with essentials and work preserved. A tie after deletion supports simpler wording only within that tested opportunity; it does not prove universal irrelevance. Failure to reproduce the initial gain leaves the clause's contribution unresolved.

Do not begin a parallel deletion search, tune on sealed/validation outputs, or remove audience, scope, precision/depth, host, timely-warning/approval or full-work safeguards to chase a preference. The next ablation question should follow the actual useful result; there is no reason to reserve multiple variants now. No publication, release, model expansion or new runtime component is proposed.
