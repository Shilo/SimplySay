# Experimental development results and decision

2026-10-29. **No tested candidate has demonstrated a consistent agreed communication benefit over absent. Stop subject dispatch at 16 of the 32 additional conversations; keep the final allocation unspent.** This is a negative/inconclusive development result, not release validation or proof that every instruction-only design fails. No production architecture or publication is recommended yet.

## What changed and actually ran

Two real independent challengers reviewed instructions/scope and cases/evaluation separately. They helped replace an unsupported confirmed-human prerequisite with a task-context audience gate and one identical factual simulation preamble in every arm. They found and corrected capture/byte/allocation defects before subjects. The instruction challenger produced two compact mechanisms, then revised both from observed behavior: A selects detail for the current question; B builds follow-ups from established context. No domain-specific timestamp or workshop exception was added.

Twelve native subjects completed 40 same-ID task turns on two realistic development conversations: operational readiness with follow-ups/copy-ready text, and an actual multi-file repair with tests, documentation and requested maintenance depth. Screening compared absent, A/B v2 enabled and the unchanged 31-word manual instruction. Revision testing compared A/B v3 against the unchanged development evidence, using the exact first three repair turns. Original full four-turn repair records and handoffs remain intact. Reused references are not fresh repetitions. The unrelated D3 proposal was frozen but never run; the targeted adjustment was independently challenged and recorded before new subjects.

All subjects used isolated neutral profiles/projects, Codex CLI 0.163.0, gpt-6.1-sol/low and the same stock host settings. No verbosity instruction, artificial delay, favorable baseline rewrite, global installation, permanent preference override, paid service or direct Claude execution was introduced. Eight enabled subjects each have one correct owned startup hash; absent/manual have none. Installed bytes, effective isolation and observed continuation passed. All 12 temporary sign-in copies were removed. Exact model attention/audience classification and effective prompt precedence remain unverified despite loading evidence.

Two independent treatment-blinded reviewers each assessed screening and revisions in their allocated continuing conversation. Sides, candidate order and pair order were reversed. All four review responses passed the unchanged four controls and strict literal-quote/schema checks directly: 16 calibrated control judgments, no conversion or reviewer retry. They saw actual files, commands, ordinary replies and progress. These are **automated assessments, not measured human comprehension**; the same two reviewers are not four fresh reviewer conversations.

| B v3 versus historical manual, repair prefix | — | Both prefer B; it avoids that detour, but still ties absent |

No A/B version meets the recorded development gate: both reviewers must identify a specific unnecessary-reading or interruption reduction over absent on at least one scenario, without a material regression/policy-specific essential loss elsewhere. One reviewer values framing passed checks separately from outstanding attention; the other considers the baseline sufficiently clear. That is not an agreed filler, jargon or interruption improvement.

Representative matched material, with all full records retained:

- **Small clarity signal:** absent labels completed checks as “other priorities”; revised replies distinguish passed checks from the sole current blocker. One reviewer prefers that framing, one ties. Do not turn the split into a demonstrated reading benefit.
- **Fewer updates, no established gain:** the absent repair has an opening and a causal diagnosis update. A v3 has only the opening; its completed work and requested later explanation remain detailed. Both reviewers still tie the communication. A count reduction is observed, but its usefulness is not established.
- **Observed avoidable detour:** manual announces that CSV escaping needs a fix, then says a direct check shows it was correct and the display was misread. The correction is necessary once the false claim is sent; the initial premature claim creates the avoidable interruption. Both reviewers prefer absent/B v3 over that run. One example does not establish the manual instruction caused the reasoning error.
- **Needed depth survives:** the maintainer request receives concrete identity, timestamp, tie/order and coverage explanations. Reviewers do not penalize useful technical terms or the substantive length. Copy-ready messages are assessed as protected standalone deliverables, not chat clutter.

All real pair judgments are retained: 32 judgments across both batches, including 23 ties. Reversed orientations do not eliminate disagreement; reviewer variation and position are confounded. Passing identity/filler/depth/omission controls does not prove absence of subtle bias or universal detector sensitivity.

## Information and work, separately

Independent inspection bound 242 actual snapshot files across 40 turns, and inspected executable sources/scripts/tests before offline probes. Requested decisions, copy-ready volunteer/staff messages, tests, maintainer documents and original full handoffs retain their required detail in inspected outputs. Actual test commands/results were inspected; test totals are not work-quality scores. No policy-specific inappropriate shortening was found in these inspected outputs. This is bounded observation, not proof of unchanged internal effort or universal scope isolation.

**Full code-contract correctness fails across conditions.** All implementations use Date.parse and collapse valid sub-millisecond timestamp instants, so an older `.0001Z` row can beat a later `.0002Z` row. The unrestricted newest-instant contract promises more. A source verifier executed the counterexample, and both blinded reviewers independently identify the same limitation from the actual code. Real upstream precision is unknown. Passing frozen offset/millisecond probes and disclosing a limitation in some handoffs do not fix the contract. The failure remains; it is not automatically a concision-caused regression and no case-specific policy rule was added to conceal it.

Original unreadable native parent-to-child instructions remain **unverified**. No blocked retest or alternative delegation setup occurred. Current development collaborators' detailed reports are real work but are not candidate-enabled native delegation trials. V3's unrequested fourth-turn handoff, structured/memory/machine-caller behavior, full inter-agent routing, fresh selected-version disabled/manual final comparisons, desktop and Claude compatibility remain unverified/deferred. Missing evidence is not a pass.

## Diagnosis and recommendation

The evidence distinguishes several live explanations. Most natural baselines already communicate clearly; the technical detail was often explicitly requested. The cases stress preservation more than the owner's sustained research/administrative narration. Stronger instructions may be ineffective or redundant with stock rules; correct loading alone cannot settle semantic adherence. Host-required opening/periodic messages can impose a floor, but do not explain every tie. No single host/model/case cause has been isolated.

A read-only inspection of this exact project's visible chat summaries found the historical workflow around the owner's complaint: bookkeeping/calibration/preparation updates alongside a genuine evidence blocker and necessary approval. That supports a target-workflow gap, not another scored absent trial. Actual excerpts/feedback stay ignored and private; [public provenance](../experiments/development-v1/complaint-context.json) contains only their hash and abstract patterns. Exact desktop model/effective prompt and message cadence are unavailable. See [workflow diagnosis](development-workflow-gap.md) and the [independent allocation challenge](development-next-allocation-challenge.md).

Recommend a different, authentic workflow experiment before more instruction text or production design. The [one proposed reallocation](development-next-allocation.md) uses the unused 16 within the same 32/108 ceilings, with bounded continuations of existing challengers/reviewers. It explicitly defers fresh disabled/manual final comparisons and needs owner approval. Keep the original broader final benchmark unchanged; narrower observations cannot be silently relabeled as qualification. No final case has been opened for content or used for tuning.
