# Final focused pilot: decision and completion

**Decision: a specific explanation-completeness defect prevents qualification under the frozen pass conditions. Stop the pilot here.** All three conditions omit a required consequence of confirming D: D+E still fails because it has too few kits and E remains late. The absent reply also omits the newly eligible A+D option. These are conversational omissions; the actual generated files and full existing agent report remain intact. Because the baseline shares the defect, this is not evidence that a style instruction caused it.

Both calibrated blinded reviewers preferred enabled and minimal over absent for explanatory usefulness, and tied enabled versus minimal. They found no advantage in filler, repetition, unexplained jargon or excessive updates. A favorable communication preference does not repair the essential omission. No condition earns an unqualified semantic-preservation pass; no production implementation or new policy is approved by this result. No additional pilot round will start automatically.

## What follows, with approval

The 31-word text remains the smallest tested candidate; the longer policy has no observed communication advantage over it. Neither qualifies as fully adequate under this round's frozen completeness requirement. Do not add procurement-specific rules or more policy text merely to repair one example, and do not present the shared baseline error as a plugin-caused regression.

The [17-item product checklist](product-completion-checklist.md) is finite and remains open. It covers approved design/audience/host scope, minimal implementation, compatibility, actual loading, protected work and inter-agent boundaries, enabled/disabled/absent behavior, installation/upgrade, disabling/removal, documentation/rollback, licensing, files and full-history privacy review, exact release approval and eventual public publication. Necessary release validation requires a separately agreed scope, session budget and stopping rule. It cannot be substituted with this pilot or silently narrowed.

Production implementation, final marketplace identities and publication remain on hold. This final pilot is finished; further work needs a concrete design/correction decision and the appropriate new approval, not routine grading or an automatic extra round.
