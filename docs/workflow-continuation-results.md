## Improved, tied or worse

**Improved previously, not newly demonstrated here:** both blinded reviewers still have their retained preference for v4's more useful progress content on the completed study workflow. That was a modest reduction in repeated previews/routine-check narration, with requested information/work preserved. This continuation neither replaces nor broadens that result.

**Tied in the continuation:** both independent reviewers tie the observed archive communication on every assessed dimension: clarity, relevance, filler, repetition, unnecessary jargon and excessive updates. Both versions give a useful repair result and preserve detailed technical work. Both also retain small finishing-guide clauses with little additional reader value. There is no demonstrated further gain in follow-ups, fewer unnecessary updates or jargon. Already-clear material can legitimately tie; the tie is not proof that the concept is universally ineffective.

**Worse evidence coverage:** a controller resume-capture defect interrupted the absent practical reply. One approved attempt was consumed; its failure was retained. The defect was diagnosed from existing logs and reproduced/fixed offline before further launches. No extra retry was granted. The final absent slot was used for the original repair/handoff request, keeping the practical reply unverified. This prevents a clean matched inference across the full follow-up sequence. It is not a model omission, style-caused regression or a successful concise answer. See [capture failure](workflow-continuation-capture-failure.md).

Both reviewers passed all four controls, including omitted-essential, added-filler, lost-depth and identical-text checks. Controls are reused, not novel holdouts. No judgment formatting conversion occurred. These are automated assessments, not measured human comprehension; both explicitly decline to credit silence, missing answers or message counts. Neither update-cadence compliance nor desktop notification reduction is established.

## Recommendation

Keep the 112-word v4 experimental. One completed workflow supports better selection of progress content; the continuation does not establish a broader communication advantage. Do not claim that filler, jargon, repetition and unnecessary interruptions are all resolved. Before production, obtain clean untouched validation with stable capture and retained work/scope checks, including fresh exact-version disabled/manual and lifecycle coverage. Do not automatically launch another round, open the sealed final conversations or change criteria to qualify.
