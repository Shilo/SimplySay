## What conflicted

The inherited reviewer instruction required exact quotes in **evidence** and explanations of omissions in **issues**. The controller's dispatch and final packet instruction incorrectly required literal sample quotations in both arrays. The unchanged omission-control check expected the reviewer to explain the specific missing action in issues.

Both reviewers had already made the substantive judgment correctly: the deliberately incomplete standalone email omitted the R-103 receipt action. Both marked its information status omitted-essential and explained that exact defect in their rationale. Their issues arrays instead quoted the incomplete email. This was an observed field-placement failure, not an absent or contrary omission judgment. No evidence shows a changed real-pair preference, information judgment or rationale caused by this conflict. Internal counterfactual reasoning is unobservable; the recovery does not claim otherwise.

The earlier report correctly retained the strict field-gate failures, but stopping at that format failure left recoverable evidence unused. The owner's latest instruction authorizes recovery without changing meaning. No reviewer-only reassessment is needed for this specific defect.

## What this tie means

This is a mixed workflow, so a single tie label must not become an all-goals pass:

| Observed part | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Initial decision and short follow-up | Both are focused, state the corrected figures and identify the next decision. The follow-up has no progress message. These are acceptable ties: further shortening is unnecessary. |
| Technical language and requested detail | Reviewers found relevant, understandable terms on both sides. Detailed methods and standalone committee text are required work, not filler. No jargon defect is demonstrated in this example; general jargon improvement remains untested. |
| Later handoff progress | Both still preview or recap artifact contents. The absent response says “I’m also spelling out how repeated tasks and shared volunteers limit what the results establish”; enabled says “The next-check plan also calls for explicit timing endpoints before collecting new records.” Reviewers found modest reader benefit and no systematic reduction in unnecessary narration. This portion is not evidence that both meet the communication goal: the target pattern remains on both sides. |
| Opening/cadence, repeated final results | Host-required presence does not prove useful content, but a complete final may properly repeat a preliminary finding. Neither all repetition nor every update is classified as unnecessary. These logs do not measure desktop notifications, reader exhaustion or comprehension. |
| Information and work | Both unchanged reviews judge preservation. Existing direct checks retain correct calculations, full requested methods, exact draft copies and source bytes. No important loss was found in inspected outputs. Original unreadable child instructions remain unverified. |

The first study pair's narrower positive finding remains historical evidence. The replication now supplies interpretable contrary evidence: that advantage did not repeat on the same workflow. This does not prove universal ineffectiveness, and it does not establish consistent added value.

## One recommendation

**Stop further trials of unchanged v4 and record that its added communication value has not been demonstrated repeatably on this workflow.** Keep it experimental. The recovered tie supplies no justification for a response rerun, another reviewer session or an automatic policy rewrite. No further experiment is proposed here. Any later development experiment needs a specific new hypothesis and approval; broader release requirements remain on the product checklist.
