## Pass conditions and stopping, fixed before launch

1. **Communication improvement:** both valid calibrated reviewers prefer enabled progress communication with concrete evidence of less optional preview/checking/finishing narration or repetition. Preference must reflect easier-to-use content, not just fewer words/messages, silence, changed work or delayed required updates. Accurate, needed findings and necessary technical detail retain credit. Already-clear turns may tie.
2. **Information/work preservation separately:** existing request-derived objective and actual-output checks pass; reviewers retain required content, reasons, uncertainty and requested depth in chat, complete files and standalone committee copy. Required approvals/blockers/warnings are not hidden to earn brevity. Inspect actual snapshots and execution; shared errors remain failures without automatic plugin attribution. No observed regression or extra omission of host-required communication may be excused as a style gain.
3. **Honest alternatives:** baseline preference or an enabled-specific loss is unfavorable. A tie with both replies already satisfactory is acceptable for that portion but no incremental gain; a tie with remaining unwanted narration leaves the problem unsolved. Invalid substantive grading, disagreement or incomplete evidence is inconclusive. Retain all outcomes. Transparent verbatim format-only recovery of existing statements is permitted as documented; never invent a missing judgment or rerun subjects because grading failed.
4. **Boundary limits:** work/code/documentation/copy-ready outputs remain protected. This isolated route has no new native child, so opaque parent-to-child instructions remain unverified; no delegation-boundary pass. Other tasks, actual UI interruption burden, human comprehension, compatibility/lifecycle, disabled/manual comparisons and release validation remain open.
5. **Stop:** no within-run wording changes, second candidate, retry, replacement, extra helper, reviewer follow-up or automatic next round. Even a favorable result is one development observation, not repeatable or product-wide proof. Freeze candidate/inputs before dispatch if approved; preserve partials and failures. If provider included-use permission or pinned settings are unavailable, stop without substitution.
