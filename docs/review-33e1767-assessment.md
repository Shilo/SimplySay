The owner's new instruction supersedes it prospectively. A specific replacement/removal with a falsifiable question is sufficient. This does not justify cycling synonyms or unchanged candidates after failure.

Fresh comparisons should use the actual new installed package with natural absent and installed-disabled controls. A historical comparison is not a matched replication. Manual instructions remain separately labelled; beating identical manual text is not necessary.
5. **Separate test questions and categories.** A short conversation can assess plain explanations, narrow follow-ups and explicitly requested depth without a work-heavy setup. A longer local audit can assess optional narration and real deliverables. A neutral-chat derivative of W1 must be labelled a familiar development case, not an independent domain or untouched validation. Keep original W1 as explicit-preference evidence.

It does not establish the whole product, nor excuse lost essentials/work or hide a worse dimension. Record each dimension and tie basis. Preserve original combined gates/results as historical; publish the prospective unit's decision rule before its outputs.

Competitor feasibility/benchmark limits remain as recorded; no instruction/persona/code-minimization text is imported.
8. **Do not turn development consumption into an invented bill.** The runtime remains small with no extra model call. Included-access task/token use is tracked; attributable dollars, latency gains and a candidate cost advantage have not been measured.
