## What changed and why

A/B v3 mainly emphasized relevance, avoiding unnecessary background and not restarting a follow-up summary. V4 uses a smaller, more concrete content-selection mechanism: put detail in the requested destination; avoid repeating artifact explanations or routine preparation/checks in progress; make updates useful through a material result, decision, blocker or meaningful wait. This targets the observed study-workflow previews and checking inventories. It adds no case fact or exception for a particular study.

The human-directed ordinary-chat limit, unclear-audience fallback, requested depth, warnings/approvals, full work/verification and preservation of files/code/docs/copy-ready/machine/tool/memory/inter-agent material remain. Those surfaces are outside the intended style scope, not opportunities to shorten work. The wording is an instruction boundary, not technical isolation; broader machine/agent scope remains unverified. Host opening/cadence requirements were retained. No host system prompt was replaced and no manual policy was supplied in these enabled comparisons. There is no evidence-based reason to revise v4 merely because the archive case tied.
