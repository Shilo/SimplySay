## Decision

Keep the112-word v4 as the next candidate, delivered by the small actual installed SessionStart package. Both independent blinded reviewers preferred it on the complete study workflow and judged essential information/work preserved. Both tied the available archive first turn and marked its later work unknown. No case-specific policy rule or final-case tuning was used. This supports a modest content-selection signal on one completed realistic conversation; it does not establish repeatable benefit, desktop interruption reduction, broad compatibility or release readiness.

The two observed native startup handlers completed in252/259ms; these observations do not establish universal latency. Do not promise zero overhead or technical audience isolation. All evaluation machinery remains outside the installed package.

Interface tool notices, hook statuses and notifications are separate from authored chat; actual interruption burden is not measured. No host system prompt or requirement was replaced.
