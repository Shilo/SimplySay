# Why the earlier candidates differed little

Date: 2026-10-29. **Offline controller analysis of existing evidence, not new independent review or a new experimental result.** The new experimental development phase is authorized in principle; its proposed additional session budget is still awaiting approval. No new helper, reviewer, subject or diagnostic conversation has run. Previous criteria and results remain intact.

The earlier evidence cannot distinguish a universally ineffective policy from a policy tested mostly where little unnecessary reading was present. The baseline was often already clear; the cases supplied little repeated long-task status; and the stock host already carries communication instructions. Native loading was observed, but execution of a hook does not prove that the model applied its audience gate or changed its behavior.

## Distinguish the explanations

| Explanation | What the existing evidence supports | What it does not establish / next discriminating check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Already-good baseline | In the first blinded packet the four reimbursement wrappers contain only 37–42 words, with one or two progress messages. All four reviewers tie every reimbursement comparison. In the final three-turn case, absent finals contain 49, 356 and 56 words, with progress counts 1/0/1. | There is no demonstrated room for meaningful shortening of those short wrappers. Do not worsen the baseline or count an appropriate tie as either efficacy or failure. Keep clear turns as safeguards. |
| Cases poorly suited to the complaints | The earlier reimbursement task primarily stresses code/files/email correctness. The queue task explicitly asks for substantial explanation. The final procurement task finishes with few updates, a strong supplied report and no elapsed cadence evidence. | A 1,371-word requested explanation is not automatically clutter. These cases barely test repeated progress across longer work or follow-ups that need only a changed answer. New neutral multi-turn repair and operational conversations should create natural opportunities, without forced verbosity or delays. |
| Instructions too broad or redundant | The 192-word policy and 31-word manual instruction both ask for clear chat and preserved work; existing stock host writing rules overlap that goal. The final reviewers tie them on communication, with all six real judgments tying on filler, repetition, jargon and excessive updates. | Ties do not isolate whether the text was too vague, already satisfied, conditionally inactive or redundant with the host. Compare two compact mechanisms: a reader-benefit gate for content/interruptions, and an incremental approach to follow-ups. Do not append case-specific exceptions. |
| Loading problems | The final owned startup audit records one SessionStart event with exactly the frozen policy hash. The actual installed bytes and trust were checked; absent and manual controls did not have the owned hook. Earlier installed-disabled evidence is separate. | Startup evidence is stronger than merely finding a file, but is not proof of attention, correct scope classification, sustained adherence or desktop loading. Bind each new arm's effective configuration, installed bytes and execution evidence; keep behavioral assessment separate. Do not repeatedly launch models to diagnose a static packaging mismatch. |
| Host-required behavior | Existing disposable profiles cache the same model-template hash. Its generic instructions explicitly require an opening commentary message and updates within 60 seconds during work. The public Codex guide also describes progress conventions, and SessionStart output is additional developer context. | A cached template is not the actual outgoing request, so exact effective delivery and precedence remain unverified. The guide's examples are not proof that this model has that exact cadence. Retain default matched host instructions and assess discretionary narration separately. A plugin must not promise to remove interruptions that the supported host requires. |
| Audience/scope gate limits application | The old policy says a user-role message alone is not proof of a human audience. The new drafts likewise restrict themselves to confirmed human chat. Current hook evidence does not expose a deterministic human-audience flag. | A safe instruction can also become inactive when an automated test does not establish a human audience. The independent challengers must examine this before trials: represent legitimate human-directed conversations consistently across arms without adding style cues or pretending machine callers are humans. Unknown audience behavior and unreadable child instructions cannot be passed by inference. |

The public [Codex prompting guide](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/gpt-5/codex_prompting_guide) supplies context about progress conventions, not proof of the installed version's effective prompt. The [hook documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/hooks) explains model-visible context and exact-definition trust. Those documents support checking both loading and host constraints; they do not establish that simply suppressing all updates is possible or desirable. CLI evidence cannot certify desktop or Claude behavior.

## The earlier completeness failure needs interpretation too

The final round's strict frozen requirement included explaining that D+E remains ineligible after D is confirmed, although the request foregrounded A+C, B+D and C+D. All three conditions failed that criterion; actual final files and all ten JSON pair rows passed, and the full source report survived. Enabled and manual also included A+D and the conditional cheapest choice, which absent omitted. Both reviewers preferred their explanation for that useful coverage while tying the clutter dimensions.

The historical failure remains recorded as such. Its significance for an ordinary reader is a legitimate rubric-scope question: “what changes if D is confirmed” can justify discussing affected alternatives, but it does not automatically mean every inferable consequence must be restated. This edge should not become a universal requirement to enumerate all facts. Neither ignoring the old criterion nor adding procurement-specific policy text would answer the user's communication complaint.

For the new phase, each essential must trace to the actual request or a concrete risk of materially misleading the reader. A sufficient answer may omit optional context. Needed explanations, uncertainty and standalone deliverable content remain mandatory. A shared baseline error is a real failure on the adopted requirement, not by itself an instruction-caused regression. Preserve separate communication and information judgments rather than allowing either to erase the other.

## Changes prepared, not demonstrated improvements

Two controller-authored drafts are available for independent challenge:

- [Candidate A](../experiments/development-v1/candidates/a-relevance-gate.v1.txt), 103 words / 724 bytes: ask whether each explanation, recap or discretionary interruption helps the reader decide, act or understand a meaningful change. This makes “routine narration” more concrete, but could wrongly suppress context or wait information if interpreted too narrowly.
- [Candidate B](../experiments/development-v1/candidates/b-incremental-conversation.v1.txt), 95 words / 715 bytes: answer the current question and supply the change or missing understanding in follow-ups. This targets rereading and repeated summaries, but could under-explain prerequisites or suppress useful repetition for a confused reader.
- The existing 31-word manual instruction remains an unchanged comparator. The old 192-word policy remains historical evidence rather than an arm repeatedly rescored without modification.

Both new drafts preserve requested depth, useful technical terms, warnings/approvals, full task effort and verification. Both exclude files, code, documentation, copy-ready deliverables, structured/tool traffic, memory and inter-agent messages from chat-only editing. Their exclusions are instructions, not verified isolation. The phrase “confirmed human audience” itself needs challenge because current host evidence may not support that predicate reliably. Do not confuse source wording with demonstrated protection.

Three [development drafts](../experiments/development-v1/cases.development.draft.json) now cover an operational decision and follow-ups, an actual local code repair with tests/notes and a practical follow-up, and requested technical depth with an actual runbook. They are not launchable cases yet: fixtures and clause-derived checks await the budgeted independent author. Two later holdouts must be independently authored and sealed before development outputs; their contents have not been written or inspected here. No result has been selected, scored again by a new model or claimed improved.

## Proposed decision and limits

Proceed with the [bounded development proposal](development-phase-plan.md) only after the owner approves its additional allocation. It reserves real independent challenges, a single revision opportunity, fresh holdouts, fixed repetitions and blinded calibrated review, using the existing tools. A provisional recommendation requires repeated, specific communication benefit over natural absent responses with preserved essentials and work. An interruption claim needs actual interruption improvement; improved prose alone cannot satisfy it. Convenience or the smallest word count cannot substitute.

If stock host requirements prevent useful reduction, identify that constraint before discussing a different architecture. If all natural baselines remain clear, the result is limited opportunity/inconclusive efficacy rather than manufactured failure or success. If protection fails, retain the actual failed artifact or message and address the general defect during development; do not hide it with a polished final summary. Existing incoming native child instructions stay **unverified** and their blocked test stays blocked. Available old child work can be inspected without claiming the missing direction was safe.

The [history lock](../experiments/development-v1/history-lock.json) covers all 287 tracked files at the completed-pilot revision `5cebead`. It preserves previous policies, criteria, tools, evidence and results while allowing additions in this separate experimental phase. No production architecture, permanent preference change, new runtime evaluator or public publication is included. Product completion still requires the separately agreed compatibility, scope, lifecycle and release work.
