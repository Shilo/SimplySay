## Why change the drafts before screening

A v1's “include each needed reason ... once” could be read as a hard anti-repetition rule even when a necessary caveat must accompany an updated recommendation. A v2 keeps necessary information and asks only whether filler, a recap or narration contributes understanding or decision value. This is still subjective and may overlap the stock host's writing instructions; it is a mechanism to test, not a guarantee. The relevance gate must not erase sources, uncertainty, limitations or a required standalone explanation just because they do not immediately change a decision.

B v1 already has the more natural conversational mechanism. B v2 retains its focus on changes and missing understanding, explicitly targets redundant introductions/conclusions, and uses the same audience/protection language as A. It does not forbid revisiting a premise or supplying a requested recap. “Include earlier context when needed for understanding,” requested depth and explicit user preferences constrain its incremental bias. A reader's confusion may justify considerable explanation.

Both versions preserve required content, detail and format in copy-ready text, quotations, files, code, documentation, structured output, tool traffic, memory and inter-agent messages. This is a categorical output boundary, not a list of case exceptions. Requested prose in chat can be a deliverable; location in a final message does not make it ordinary conversation. A full requested explanation to the reader remains inside chat scope but retains its requested depth. Both versions explicitly retain full work and verification, so the mechanism is editing communication rather than reducing research or task completion.

## Mechanism tests and key risks

| Candidate | Predicted improvement if useful | Failure signal or limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A v2: relevance gate | Removes an unnecessary recap, filler or routine progress interruption while keeping the answer, needed context and meaningful change clear. | A shorter reply that loses a caveat, explanation or useful expectation is a preservation failure, not a win. If only required cadence messages remain, interruption reduction may be constrained by the host. |
| B v2: incremental conversation | Later turns answer the new question or explain the changed situation without reprinting unchanged background; confusion triggers useful explanation. | Omitting needed prior context, repeating stale recommendations, or treating a requested comprehensive recap as disposable defeats the mechanism. First-turn improvements alone do not demonstrate the intended follow-up benefit. |
| Both | Ordinary chat improves while actual protected outputs and full work remain adequate. | The exclusion list is model instruction, not technical isolation. A copy-ready email or runbook shortened inside chat is still a protected-output failure. Tool/agent/memory scope remains unproven unless actually observed. |

The policies remain close to native clear-writing defaults. A natural absent baseline may meet both mechanisms already; ties would be informative, not an excuse to worsen the baseline or keep searching without a promising observed defect. Extra policy words and packaging convenience are costs/benefits separate from demonstrated communication efficacy. Neither draft promises measured comprehension, model diversity, desktop behavior, Claude compatibility or release readiness.
